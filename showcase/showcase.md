# Carom

Carom is a bright, fast paddle duel played across a midnight-blue arena. Every
rally starts with a clean serve and quickly turns into a contest of position,
timing, and nerve.

Move as you strike to put spin on the ball. The curve lingers after contact,
making a familiar return become a surprising arc at the far paddle.

The amber center bars are more than scenery: use them for sharp bank shots, or
watch an overconfident angle come racing back at you. Reach eleven points and
lead by two to take the match.
